rythmeet
mdc praxis

streamlining user experience through simplified flows

timeline:

spring 2026

role:

primary designer

team:

julia lau, kelly chen, naisha srivastava, stephanie lin, amber lee, catherine mao, johanna kim, katie li, yeji moon

project scope

the goal:

to streamline rythmeet's user experience by simplifying user flows and establishing consistent ui across the website

week 1-2

website design critique

our designers started by evaluating the current state of rythmeet's website and presented their critiques with a focus on user flows of the sign-up process, editing profile, creating events, and accessing dashboard stats.

based on their critiques, designers presented their proposed user flow diagram that would streamline the user experience. due to time constraints, we initially established our project scope to be improving the create profile user flow and create events user flow.

week 3-6

user flow re-design & prototyping

next, designers finalized the improved user flow diagrams and began prototyping these changes with mid to high fidelity wireframes, ensuring consistency and simplicity were maintained throughout all pages.

sign-up flow

the updated sign-up flow was improved by consolidating sign-up steps, including an animated visual progress indicator, and maintaining a modular, clean yet unique ui to the onboarding process.

create/edit events flow

the updated create/edit events user flow was improved by placing emphasis on being able to quickly create and edit events. to achieve this, we utilized a pop-up window from the original page, as opposed to being redirected to new page, and streamlined the options event organizers could choose.

week 7-8

dashboard re-design & branding

with our project scope completed, our team broke off into two teams to tackle two more projects: to continue re-design process of the dashboard & to update rythmeet's branding guidelines to elevate its current state.

dashboard redesign

the professional essence of the original dashboard was preserved, but our main goal was to allow the user to easily switch between the different dashboards for a different role (e.g. musician to band). we also introduced tabs, reduced the amount of metrics displayed per tab, and changed it to be an at-a-glance, non-scrollable page.

updated branding guidelines

we created an updated branding guideline that included more pastel colors and suggests incorporating gradients as a new part of their brand identity that promotes the idea of harmony and of connecting users over their shared connection of music.

impact & learnings

this project taught us the value of iterative design—starting with critique, moving through structured user flow improvements, and finishing with a cohesive branding system. working across a large, cross-functional team required clear communication and modular design thinking to keep every page consistent.

by focusing on simplification at every step—from onboarding to dashboards—we helped rythmeet create a more intuitive, harmonious experience that reflects its mission of connecting people through music.
